# Dal Tadka

## Base
Toor dal 250g, pressure cooked 4 whistles with turmeric and salt.

## Tadka
Ghee 2 tbsp, cumin 1 tsp, garlic 6 cloves sliced, dried red chilies 2,
tomatoes 2 chopped, ginger julienne. Pour sizzling over the dal, finish
with coriander. Staple for the Sunday prep in [[meal-plan]].
